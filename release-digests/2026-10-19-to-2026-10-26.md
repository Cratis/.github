Assigned identities, the top-level model revision and other provenance are normalized, so reassigning ids never creates a false difference. Literal text, patterns, labels and messages remain significant, and arrays compare as sets or ordered according to their schema path. Unknown arrays and unresolved identities fail explicitly. The rules are documented in https://github.com/Cratis/Screenplay/blob/main/Documentation/screenplay/semantic-conformance.md

(#1064)
