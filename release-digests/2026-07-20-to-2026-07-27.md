- **AuthProxy** ([Release v2.7.0](https://github.com/Cratis/AuthProxy/releases/tag/v2.7.0)): `GET /.cratis/logout` — signs the user out of the authentication cookie and clears every AuthProxy session cookie (`.cratis-identity`, `.cratis-tenant`, `.cratis-tenants`, `.cratis-invite`, `.cratis-registration`, `.cratis-providers`), then redirects to the absolute URL in the `redirect` query parameter.
